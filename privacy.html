1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how FH Development ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data when you visit our website, purchase our products, engage our services, or otherwise interact with us. It applies wherever you are located, but describes additional rights available to you specifically if you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, or India.

For the purposes of the UK GDPR, the data controller of your personal data is FH Development Ltd, registered in England and Wales under company number [UK Company Number], registered office at [UK Registered Address]. For the purposes of the DPDPA, the "Data Fiduciary" in respect of personal data collected from users in India is FH Development India Private Limited, CIN [India CIN], registered office at [India Registered Address]. Where we jointly determine the purposes and means of processing, both entities act as joint controllers/fiduciaries and either may be contacted using the details in Section 20.

3. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity data: name, username, date of birth (where relevant to age verification), and profile information.
  • Contact data: email address, phone number, billing and postal address.
  • Account data: login credentials, account preferences, and communication history with us.
  • Transaction data: details of products or services you have purchased, order history, and payment confirmation (we do not store full card numbers — these are processed directly by our payment processor).
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, device identifiers, operating system, and other technology on the devices you use to access our website.
  • Usage data: information about how you use our website, products, and services, including pages visited and features used.
  • Marketing & communications data: your preferences in receiving marketing from us and your communication preferences.
  • Project data: specifications, brand assets, source files, and other materials you provide in connection with a custom development or design engagement.

We do not intentionally collect special category data (such as health information, religious beliefs, or biometric data) or "sensitive personal data" as defined under applicable law, unless you voluntarily provide it to us and it is strictly necessary for the Services, in which case we will seek your explicit consent where required.

4. How We Collect Information

We collect information: (a) directly from you, when you create an account, place an order, contact support, or communicate with us; (b) automatically, through cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy; and (c) from third parties, such as payment processors, analytics providers, and, where applicable, Roblox or other platforms you connect to our Services.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases: performance of a contract with you; compliance with a legal obligation; our legitimate interests (such as fraud prevention, service improvement, and direct marketing to existing customers), provided these are not overridden by your interests or fundamental rights; and, where required, your consent (for example, for non-essential cookies or marketing communications), which you may withdraw at any time.

Under the DPDPA, we process personal data on the basis of your consent (which you may give, refuse, or withdraw at any time via clear affirmative action) or, where applicable, "legitimate uses" recognised under the Act, such as for the specified purpose for which you have voluntarily provided your data, compliance with law, or responding to a medical or safety emergency.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use personal data to: provide, operate, and maintain the Services; process orders and payments; deliver custom development and design work; provide customer support; send transactional communications (e.g. order confirmations, service updates); with your consent or on a legitimate interest basis, send marketing communications; improve, personalise, and secure our website and Services; detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; comply with legal, regulatory, and tax obligations; and enforce our Terms of Service and other policies.

7. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We may share personal data with:

  • Service providers and processors who perform functions on our behalf, such as payment processing, cloud hosting, analytics, and customer support tooling, under contractual obligations of confidentiality and data protection.
  • Professional advisers, including lawyers, auditors, and insurers, where necessary for their services to us.
  • Regulators and law enforcement, where required by law, court order, or governmental request, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users or the public.
  • A buyer or successor, in the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganisation, or sale of assets, subject to equivalent protections being maintained.
  • Third parties with your consent, such as where you ask us to share information with Roblox or another platform to deliver the Services.

8. International Data Transfers

Because FH Development operates between the United Kingdom and India, and may use service providers located elsewhere, your personal data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than your own. Where we transfer personal data out of the UK, we rely on adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, as applicable. Under the DPDPA, cross-border transfers of personal data are generally permitted except to countries specifically restricted by the Central Government of India, and we will observe any such restrictions as they are notified.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements (for example, UK tax records are generally retained for six years, and Indian statutory records in line with the Companies Act 2013 and applicable tax legislation). Where you close your account or withdraw consent, we will delete or anonymise your personal data unless retention is required for legal or legitimate business purposes such as dispute resolution.

10. Data Security

We implement technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, as described further in our Security Policy. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights — UK/EU

If you are located in the UK or EEA, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten"); restrict or object to certain processing (including direct marketing); request data portability; withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent; and lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or your local EEA supervisory authority. We will respond to verified requests within one month, extendable by a further two months for complex requests, as permitted under the UK GDPR.

12. Your Rights — India

If you are located in India, under the DPDPA you have the right to: obtain a summary of the personal data we process about you and the processing activities undertaken; request correction, completion, updating, or erasure of your personal data; withdraw consent at any time, as easily as it was given; nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity; and file a complaint with us in the first instance, and thereafter with the Data Protection Board of India, once its grievance-handling mechanisms are operational. We will make reasonable efforts to respond to such requests within the timeframes prescribed by applicable rules once they take full effect.
