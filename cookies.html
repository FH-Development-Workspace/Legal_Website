1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how FH Development ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website, and the choices available to you. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

2. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function, work more efficiently, and provide information to site owners. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, tags, and software development kits (SDKs) embedded in apps; references to "cookies" in this policy include these related technologies unless stated otherwise.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly necessary cookies: required for the website to function (e.g. session management, security, load balancing). These cannot be switched off and do not require consent under PECR.
  • Performance & analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g. pages visited, time spent) so we can improve it. These are set only with your consent.
  • Functionality cookies: remember choices you make (such as language or region) to provide a more personalised experience.
  • Targeting & advertising cookies: used to deliver relevant advertising and measure the effectiveness of ad campaigns, where applicable. These are set only with your consent.

4. First-Party & Third-Party Cookies

"First-party" cookies are set directly by our website. "Third-party" cookies are set by domains other than ours, for example analytics or advertising providers whose technology we embed. Third parties are responsible for their own cookies and privacy practices in respect of the data such cookies collect.

6. Legal Basis & Consent

Under PECR and the UK GDPR, we will only set non-essential cookies (performance, functionality, and advertising cookies) with your prior consent, obtained via our cookie banner or preference centre. You may withdraw consent at any time. Under India's DPDPA, similar consent principles apply to the collection of personal data through cookies and similar technologies, and consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional, and capable of being withdrawn as easily as it was given.

7. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage or withdraw your cookie consent at any time using our cookie preference tool (where available on this site), or through your browser settings, which allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Guidance on managing cookies in popular browsers is available directly from the browser vendors (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

8. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There is currently no universally accepted standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals, and our website does not currently respond to them differently from a visitor who has not enabled DNT. We will revisit this position as industry standards develop.
