1. Our Commitment

FH Development implements administrative, technical, and organisational security measures designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of user data and our infrastructure, proportionate to the nature and sensitivity of the data we process, in line with Article 32 of the UK GDPR and applicable Indian information security requirements under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2. Organisational Measures

  • Access to systems and data is restricted on a need-to-know, least-privilege basis.
  • Team members and contractors are subject to confidentiality obligations and receive periodic security awareness guidance.
  • We maintain internal procedures for provisioning and de-provisioning access when roles change or engagements end.

3. Technical Measures

  • Encryption of data in transit (e.g. TLS/HTTPS) and, where appropriate, at rest.
  • Use of strong authentication controls, including multi-factor authentication for administrative access where available.
  • Regular monitoring and logging to detect unauthorised access or anomalous activity.
  • Reasonable measures to keep software, dependencies, and infrastructure up to date with security patches.
  • Segregation of production, staging, and development environments where practicable.

No system can be guaranteed 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong, unique passwords and to enable any additional security features made available to you.

4. Breach Response & Notification

We maintain an internal incident response process to identify, contain, and remediate security incidents. Where a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms, we will notify the UK Information Commissioner's Office without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware, and will notify affected individuals where the risk is high, consistent with the UK GDPR. Where a cybersecurity incident falls within the categories specified by the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In), we will report it to CERT-In within six hours of noticing or being brought to notice of the incident, as required by CERT-In's directions issued under the Information Technology Act, 2000, and will comply with applicable DPDPA breach-notification requirements as they come into force.

5. Responsible Disclosure Programme

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability affecting our Services, we encourage you to report it responsibly by emailing hello@fh-development.xyz with sufficient detail to reproduce the issue. Provided you: (a) do not access, modify, or delete data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability; (b) do not disrupt our Services or degrade their availability to others; (c) do not publicly disclose the issue before we have had a reasonable opportunity to address it; and (d) act in good faith, we will not pursue legal action against you for that research. We aim to acknowledge vulnerability reports within 72 hours.

6. Your Security Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of any credentials issued to you, using strong and unique passwords, enabling multi-factor authentication where offered, and promptly notifying us of any suspected unauthorised access to your account.

7. Third-Party & Sub-Processor Security

Where we use third-party service providers (such as hosting, payment processing, or analytics providers) to deliver the Services, we take reasonable steps to select providers with appropriate security standards and to bind them to contractual confidentiality and security obligations consistent with this policy and our Privacy Policy.

8. Business Continuity

We maintain reasonable backup and recovery procedures for critical systems and data to reduce the impact of hardware failure, data loss, or service disruption, proportionate to the size and nature of our business.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Security Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, infrastructure, or applicable law.

10. Report a Vulnerability / Contact Us

To report a security concern or vulnerability, email hello@fh-development.xyz. We aim to acknowledge reports within 72 hours.
