1. Introduction

This Refund Policy explains when and how you may be entitled to a refund for products or services purchased from FH Development. It applies in addition to, and does not limit, any statutory right you have as a consumer under UK or Indian law.

2. Digital Products & Downloadable Assets

Digital products, templates, scripts, and downloadable assets are, as a general rule, non-refundable once the download link has been provided, the file has been delivered, or access has been granted, because such content cannot practically be "returned." Where a digital product is materially defective, does not match its description, or otherwise fails to meet applicable statutory quality standards, you remain entitled to a repair, replacement, or refund as required by law.

3. Custom Development Services & Deposits

For bespoke development, design, or consulting engagements: deposits paid to secure a project slot or to commence discovery/scoping work are generally non-refundable once work has begun, reflecting time and resources already committed. Where a project is cancelled part-way through: (a) you will be invoiced for, and remain liable to pay, the value of work completed and any non-cancellable third-party costs incurred up to the date of cancellation; and (b) any balance of an advance payment exceeding that value will be refunded to you within a reasonable time. Specific milestone and refund terms for an engagement will be set out in the applicable Order or statement of work, which will prevail over this general policy to the extent of any conflict.

4. Subscriptions & Recurring Services

Where Services are offered on a subscription or recurring basis, you may cancel future renewals at any time via your account settings or by contacting us, with cancellation taking effect at the end of the then-current billing period. Except as required by applicable consumer law, fees already paid for the current billing period are non-refundable.

5. UK Cancellation Rights

If you are a consumer in the UK and purchase Services at a distance (e.g. online), the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 generally give you a 14-day "cooling-off" period to cancel for a full refund. However, this right does not apply, or is lost, where: (a) the service has been fully performed with your prior express consent and acknowledgement that you lose the right to cancel once performance begins; or (b) you download or stream digital content with your prior express consent and acknowledgement that your cancellation right is thereby lost. Where applicable, we will ask you to provide this consent and acknowledgement before delivering digital content or beginning bespoke work within the 14-day period.

6. India Consumer Protections

If you are a consumer in India, you are entitled to the protections set out in the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, including our obligation to clearly display our refund, return, and cancellation policy, exchange procedures, and grievance redressal mechanism, and to not impose cancellation charges that are not proportionate. Nothing in this policy limits any right you have to seek redress before the appropriate Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

7. Non-Refundable Items

  • Fees for work already performed or milestones already delivered and accepted.
  • Third-party costs incurred on your behalf (e.g. licences, plugins, asset purchases, platform fees) that are non-refundable to us.
  • Digital products or source files after download, access, or delivery, except where defective or as required by law.
  • Amounts explicitly designated as non-refundable in the applicable Order.

8. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

We ask that you contact us first to resolve any billing concern before initiating a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. Fraudulent, bad-faith, or unwarranted chargebacks may result in suspension of your account, recovery of associated fees, and, where the underlying work has already been delivered, may be treated as a breach of these Terms and pursued accordingly.

9. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email hello@fh-development.xyz with your order number, the product or service purchased, the date of purchase, and the reason for your request. We aim to acknowledge refund requests within 2 business days and to resolve them within 5–7 business days.

10. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase, unless otherwise agreed. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take an additional 5–10 business days for a refund to appear in your account after it has been processed on our end.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes will apply to purchases made after the updated policy is published; refund rights for purchases made before that date will be governed by the policy in effect at the time of purchase, unless we agree otherwise or applicable law requires otherwise.

12. Contact Us

For any refund-related query, contact hello@fh-development.xyz. We respond within 5–7 business days.
